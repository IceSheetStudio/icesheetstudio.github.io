Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game "Bai Mahjong" ("the Game") handles information. The Game is developed and published by independent developer Ge Hong and operates primarily on iOS and iPadOS platforms.

1. Information Collection and Use

We are committed to providing a simple and privacy-respecting gaming experience.

  • No Direct Collection of Personal Information: We do not directly collect, store, or upload personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.
  • Local Storage: Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services

To support advertisements and certain platform features, the Game integrates the following third-party services:

Apple Game Center

  • Used for leaderboards and achievements.
  • Data such as nickname and scores are managed by Apple.
  • We do not have access to your Apple ID password or private Apple account information.

Google AdMob

  • May collect device identifiers (including IDFA with user permission), IP address, and ad interaction data.
  • Used for ad delivery, frequency control, fraud prevention, and performance measurement.
  • Operates in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Unity Ads

  • May collect device identifiers, IP address, advertising identifiers (subject to user consent), and interaction data.
  • Used for ad delivery, analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Operates in accordance with Unity’s Privacy Policy:

https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

AppLovin (MAX / Advertising)

  • May collect device identifiers, IP address, advertising identifiers (such as IDFA with user consent), and interaction data.

  • Operates in accordance with AppLovin’s Privacy Policy:

https://www.applovin.com/privacy/

3. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS devices, the Game supports Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. If you decline tracking authorization, advertising identifiers such as IDFA will not be accessed for cross-app tracking purposes.

4. GDPR (European Economic Area Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to:

  • Request access to personal data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Withdraw consent at any time

You may contact us regarding any data protection requests.

5. CCPA (California Residents)

California residents may request disclosure of categories of personal information collected and request deletion under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

6. Children’s Privacy

This Game is a casual game intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Data Security

We do not operate our own cloud servers for user data storage. Since personal data is not stored on our servers, the risk of server-side data breaches from our end is minimized.

Game data stored locally on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game.

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future to reflect new features or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
